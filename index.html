Owners ask us

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

Do I have to change my POS?

No. RestoServe works with Square, Toast, Clover, SpotOn and other POS systems. We set up the connection for your system, and every order reaches your kitchen.

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. Calls your staff don’t pick up forward to your AI host, so customers keep calling the number they know.

What if a caller wants a real person?

The AI connects them to your manager. Complaints, refunds and catering always go to you, with the details emailed so nothing is lost.

How do customers pay?

At pickup, like today. On Pro, the AI can also text a secure payment link. It never takes card numbers over the phone.

What if we run out of a dish?

Mark it sold out in your POS, or send us a quick message, and the AI stops offering it within minutes.

What languages does it speak?

55 languages, including English, Spanish, Chinese, French, Vietnamese, Arabic, Korean and Hindi. It switches as soon as the caller does, and every order reaches your kitchen in English.

How fast can we go live?

Usually within 48 hours of getting your menu, with test calls together before it answers real customers.

What happens if we go over our minutes?

Calls are never cut off. Extra minutes are billed per minute, and we alert you at 80% and 100%. If you go over often, we’ll move you to the plan that saves you money.

Are calls recorded?

Yes, so orders are accurate. The AI tells callers at the start. You can listen to your calls in your dashboard, and recordings are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Is there a contract?

Plans run for 12 months, billed monthly or yearly. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no card needed.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026. RestoServe AI provides AI phone answering and ordering for restaurants. This policy explains what we collect when you call a restaurant that uses our service, and when you contact us through this website.

What we collect

When you call, we collect your phone number, the name you give, your order details, pickup time, and the call recording and transcript. We do not collect payment card numbers by phone. When you use a form on this website, we collect the details you enter.

How we use it

We use this information only to place and confirm your order with the restaurant, to send order-related messages you request, to remember your previous order if you call again, to respond to your inquiry, and to improve the accuracy of the service.

Call recordings

Calls are recorded and transcribed so orders are accurate. The restaurant you called can listen to its own calls. Recordings are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Text messages

If you agree to receive text messages, we send only messages about your order, such as confirmations and payment links. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. We do not share your phone number or text-messaging consent with third parties for marketing.

Sharing

We share order information with the restaurant you called and with service providers that run the service (such as phone, AI and point-of-sale providers). We do not sell personal information.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: hello@restoserve.ai.

Terms of service
