How it works

Your restaurant keeps its number. Calls your staff can’t pick up go to your AI host, which knows your menu, prices and specials.

  1. A customer calls

    Your AI host answers in seconds, in English, Hindi or Urdu, and answers questions about the menu, hours and specials.

  2. The order is confirmed

    It takes the items, spice levels and pickup time, repeats the order back and confirms the name and phone number.

  3. The ticket prints

    The order goes into Square and prints in your kitchen, clearly marked as a phone order, just like an online order.

What we collect

When you call, we collect your phone number, the name you give, your order details, pickup time and the call recording and transcript. We do not collect payment card numbers by phone.

How we use it

We use this information only to place and confirm your order with the restaurant, to send order-related messages you request, and to improve the accuracy of the service.

Text messages

If you agree to receive text messages, we send only messages about your order, such as confirmations and payment links. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. We do not share your phone number or consent with third parties for marketing.
